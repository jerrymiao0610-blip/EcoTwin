# EcoTwin — Climate-Aware Campus Energy Digital Twin

## Hackathon
NextStep Hacks 2026

## Product Goal
EcoTwin helps students and schools understand how classroom decisions affect
electricity consumption, carbon emissions, and operating cost.

Users should be able to configure a virtual classroom, simulate its energy use,
optimize its configuration, and compare the current state with a more
sustainable state.

## Core Experience
Configure → Simulate → Optimize → Compare

## Required Features
1. Classroom configurator
2. Energy simulation engine
3. Energy / CO2 / cost metrics
4. Optimization engine
5. Current vs optimized comparison
6. Weather-aware simulation
7. Polished interactive dashboard

## Product Principles
- Results should change instantly when inputs change.
- Calculations must be deterministic and explainable.
- Do not fake AI or environmental data.
- Clearly distinguish estimates from measured real-world data.
- Keep the interface visual and easy to demo in under 4 minutes.

## Out of Scope
- Authentication
- Database
- Payments
- Generic chatbot
- Native mobile app
- Training a machine-learning model
- Complex 3D rendering
- IoT hardware

## Technical Stack

- React

- Tailwind CSS
